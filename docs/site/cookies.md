# Cookie policy

*Last updated: 25 November 2026.*

nosorry uses only cookies that the service cannot work without. They need no consent under EU rules; the banner shown
on your first visit is here to tell you, not to ask.

| Cookie | What it does | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| `__Host-ns_session` | keeps you signed in (a random token; the server stores only its hash) | 30 days at most, ends after 14 days without use or when you sign out |
| `ns_setup` | hands your first API key to the setup page right after sign-up, once | 10 minutes |

Your browser also keeps, in local storage, that you closed the information banner. Nothing is used for advertising,
analytics or tracking, and no third party sets cookies on nosorry.

You can delete cookies in your browser at any time; you will simply be signed out. Questions: see the
[Privacy policy](/privacy).
